# Level of use: how much to see

The program can do a lot, and you do not need all of it on the first day. The menu has a small switch, **"I am a ..."**, that decides how much of the menu and of the settings you see. The names are a joke on purpose: nobody should feel silly for starting at the bottom, and nobody has to know what an "LLM" is to use the library.

| Level | For whom | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| **Bookworm** | "I just want to read and ask questions" | Home, reading, searching, the link map, collections, learning by question, writing. In Settings: the model, backups and updates. |
| **Librarian** (the default) | "I add books and look after the shelves" | Everything above, plus adding articles, areas, the log, the setup check, privacy, the password and the reading archive. |
| **Keeper of the Scrolls** | "Show me every switch" | Everything, including safe mode, the site address and the tests. |

- The level **only hides menu items**. Every address still opens its page, and nothing is switched off or lost when you change level.
- You can change it at any time in the menu, or on the Welcome page.
- The choice is kept in this browser, so two people on two computers can use different levels.
