# Reading and searching

## Menu

- **Home**: new files, latest changes.
- **Library**: Index (a catalogue of everything), Log (what was changed and when), Link map, Areas.
- **Collections**: pages by type (Sources, Chapters, Concepts, People, Topics, Comparisons, Questions), by tag, and your own lists.
- **Tags**: a quick filter by topic.

## Search

**Ctrl K** (⌘ K on a Mac) opens search by title, aliases and text. It also finds different spellings of one name when they are saved as aliases.

## A page

- `[[links]]` lead to other pages. A red dashed link means a page that does not exist yet.
- Citations like **p. 53** are links: they open the reader at the exact PDF page of the original text. For EPUB it is "sec. N".
- A double click on a title renames it (the file and the links stay the same).
- The **Focus** button hides the bars for calm reading; **Esc** brings them back.
- Selected text gives a bubble with actions: Ask, Edit, With AI, Link, Remove ([see Fixes](#/docs/organize)).

## The log

It shows entries by day: kind (processing, upgrade, synthesis, fix, check), which pages are new and which were updated. It does not change the file.

## The map

The graph of links: drag, zoom with the wheel, click a page to open it.

## Reading offline, elsewhere

**Settings → Archive for reading** makes one ZIP with the interface and the pages, opened by double-clicking `index.html`, with no server and no internet ([Backups and archive](#/docs/backup)).
